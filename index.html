ABOUT

Dell's Independent Living provides furnished independent living housing in Charlotte, North Carolina, and surrounding communities.

Our homes offer a comfortable, respectful, and welcoming environment where residents can enjoy independence in a safe home-like setting.

Proudly Serving Seniors and Veterans Throughout Mooresville, Statesville, Troutman, Salisbury, and the Greater Charlotte Metropolitan Area of North Carolina.

Housing accommodations only. We do not provide medical care, assisted living services, personal care, medication management, meals, or transportation.
