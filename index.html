FAQS

Questions, answered.

Do you provide assisted living?

No. Dell's Independent Living provides housing accommodations only. We do not provide assisted living, medical care, personal care, medication management, meals, or transportation.

Are utilities included?

Yes. Utilities are included with the housing accommodations.

Are rooms furnished?

Yes. We offer furnished private and shared rooms.

Do you accept referrals?

Yes. We accept referrals from social workers, case managers, hospitals, churches, community organizations, family members, and referral agencies.
